## Crimson Workbench 0.9.1

**[Download the Windows installer](https://github.com/ArasHueseyin/crimson-workbench/releases/download/v0.9.1/Crimson-Workbench_0.9.1_x64-setup.exe)** · One setup includes Workbench, the loader and all optional modules.

The Marketplace grows from **7 to 35 cards**, with mod search, clearer controls and live time/weather, photo and teleport panels.

### Player movement and abilities

- **No fall damage:** protects supported player characters and rideable definitions from fall damage.
- **Unlimited player stamina & spirit:** independently preserve stamina, spirit or both. Native ability eligibility and minimum-resource requirements remain.
- **Player jump height:** adjust ordinary jump height from 100% to 300%.
- **Player movement speed:** adjust ground walking, running and sprinting from 100% to 300%.
- **Unlimited Force Palm jumps:** remove the three-use aerial chain limit. The ability must be learned; normal spirit rules remain unless separately changed.
- **Force Palm jump height:** choose a separate aerial jump-height multiplier from 1× to 100×.
- **Axiom armlet reach:** choose 2× to 5× reach, or reset to normal 1×. Native target eligibility and weight restrictions remain.
- **Gliding speed:** adjust gliding from normal speed to 500% (5×).

### Mounts

- **Larger Rokade:** make Rokade 30% larger with a dedicated appearance, leaving other horses unchanged.
- **Higher lion & Rokade jumps:** increase their jump height by 40%.
- **Stay mounted on longer falls:** raise the automatic fall-dismount threshold to 5× normal. Combat throws and manual dismounts retain their native rules.
- **Protect active mount:** protect the ridden or selected summoned mount from combat damage. Scripted deaths and despawns retain their native behavior.

### Inventory and equipment

- **999 inventory slots:** expand player inventory capacity to 999 slots.
- **Item stack limits:** search for an item, see its icon and set a per-item stack limit.
- **No durability loss:** prevent ordinary equipment wear without repairing existing damage.
- **Preserve selected consumables:** choose from 561 supported arrows, stat potions, foods and Holy Water items to retain after normal use. Effects and cooldowns remain native.
- **Native weapon effects:** transfer supported lightning or fire effects from existing weapons to compatible targets.

### Crafting and upgrades

- **Free crafting materials:** remove material requirements from supported normal crafting recipes, with up to 40 outputs per batch. Tools and recipe unlocks remain required.
- **Free equipment upgrades:** remove supported upgrade material and refinement-token costs. Existing equipment, tools and unlocks remain required.
- **No Clockwork Insect crafting fee:** remove the specific 300-Clockwork-Insect fee from the Kuku drone backpack recipe. Material costs are controlled separately.

### Progression, exploration and convenience

- **Contribution points:** increase six existing faction contribution balances to chosen target amounts.
- **Reveal world map:** reveal map fog while preserving quest, knowledge and discovery records.
- **More time beyond restricted regions:** extend the return countdown from 30 seconds to 30 minutes. Other boundaries and quest timers retain their rules.
- **Auto-loot nearby items:** collect eligible free ground items and normal corpse loot within the normal interaction radius. Ownership, crime and inventory checks remain active.
- **Automatic fishing:** add native automatic reeling to all four supported rods. Casting remains manual.
- **Live time & weather:** set or advance game time by hours; adjust rain, snow, clouds, humidity, wind and fog; freeze time/weather independently and reset the weather override.
- **Extended photo mode:** use the native movable photo camera, control HUD visibility and pause the scene. Native camera limits remain.
- **Teleport:** store 16 positions for returns within the current field, or travel to eligible mainland map pins and waypoints. Native ground and placement checks remain.

### Other improvements

- **Selective skill unlocks:** choose individual abilities or unlock all 140 main-character skills.
- Mod search with category/status filters, availability labels and per-card progress/errors.
- Exact numeric inputs, presets, retained drafts, consistent save selection and accessible dialogs.
- Localized item names and icons in the new item selectors; Item Picker replaces the separate Item Database view.
- Improved mod installation/status handling and matching-module connection checks.

The existing larger-lion, faster-mount, mount-stamina, sleep and Blackstar mods remain included, alongside Item Picker and Extra Sockets.

**When to use:** Item Picker and connected time/weather, photo and teleport panels work while playing after initial module setup. Installation, archive/save edits and startup-setting changes require a closed game.

**Compatibility:** Windows x64, supported game EXE **1.0.0.2976** and matching game data. New features passed automated checks; broader in-game acceptance remains pending. When upgrading base runtime modules, remove the old version with its original package's uninstaller first. Personal socket data is preserved.

Built and tested by GitHub Actions. **SHA256SUMS.txt** accompanies the downloads. Windows binaries are unsigned, so Windows may show an unknown publisher warning. Game assets, saves and private developer tools are excluded.
